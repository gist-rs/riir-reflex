**Distilled for the stack (modelless, measurement-side):**

## 1. What the repo is (measured facts at the pinned sha)

OntoSmith (Python ≥3.11, MIT, Neo4j-backed) builds knowledge graphs from document corpora under a **flat extraction contract** ("ontology" = closed inventory of entity/relation types with definitions + boundary examples — explicitly NOT OWL: no hierarchy, no axioms, no inference; "the flatness is the point: judged per-type decisions are what make a build's quality measurable"). The stack: `profile` (deterministic corpus census, no LLM, hash-pinned) → `ontology` (draft/validate/approve/emit loop emitting a versioned sha256-pinned **pack** only on human approval bound to the draft hash) → `batch` (eight-stage build) → `inspect` → `verify` → `search`/`serve`.

**The judgment architecture (§3.2 of their whitepaper, honestly stated):** the proposer seat (claude-haiku-5.5 in the reference profile) has no write authority; deterministic code gates check span verification (claimed evidence quote must occur verbatim), schema conformance, duplicates; the **Jev judge seat is `typesafe/jev-1.13`, treated like a compiler version — pinned, named in every run record, changed only as a product decision**; a human review band (scores 0.25–0.50 under the flagship calibration) is a designed seat, its width a measured parameter; frontier escalation exists in the harness as a bounded instrument (band-only, judging roles excluded) and is off by default in the product. One honest nuance they state plainly: the pair judge that converts enumerated pairs into candidate triples runs ON the proposer seat (same model, second fitted config) — so the enforced invariant is not "the proposer never judges" but "**nothing is written on the proposing seat's say-so alone**."

**The five Jev gates** (`pipeline/decisions.py`) are all decision_wire-shaped — the same `choice`/`score`/`noul` grammar reflex serves (their `tests/golden/v0_{choice,noul,score}.json` confirm; `decide()` posts `{model, state, questions}`):
- `er_judge` (noul): do two mentions denote the same real-world entity? Merge floor `p_same ≥ 0.85` guards name-only merges.
- `validate_triple` (noul, v1/v2): does the document support (A | rel | B), v2 adding direction emphasis?
- `choice_gate` (choice): which relation holds A→B, with a `none` option appended.
- `ontology_judge` (noul): admit an axiom (subclass/disjoint/domain/range) to the ontology? — the schema-evolution gate.
- `conflict_judge` (choice): **duplicate / contradiction / update / novel** — where `update` (relation can change over time: employer, residence) vs `contradiction` (single-valued: birthplace) is a bitemporal classification made by the judge at write time.

## 2. The mechanisms worth keeping (distilled)

### 2.1 Gold-support ceiling probe + the fate census (→ reflex Issue 108)

Their quality record prices, separately:

- **The fate census (§5.3):** every one of the 17,284 gold triples gets exactly ONE fate from a closed set — matched / emission_blocked / none_judged / never_enumerated / roster_missed — with per-stratum machine-adjudicated text-support rates + CIs. 57.9% of gold was never enumerated at all; the anatomy keeps the two recall ceilings separate: **enumeration coverage** (what the pipeline could reach) vs **gold's own as-typed support** (ĝ_B = 0.5244 — only ~52% of gold survives as typed under their ruled contract, so even perfect enumeration could not be credited past that ceiling). Per-stratum sample rates with intervals, controls shuffled in blind and reported beside-not-inside the estimate.
- **The adjudication contract loop (ADJUDICATION.md):** blind judging, as-typed questions, verbatim-quote verification (quote-failed supported verdicts recorded *unknown*, excluded from every denominator with counts published), planted blind controls beside-not-inside, verdict vocabulary `supported/not_supported/unclear` — and the **self-correction arc**: the original contract scored corrected precision 0.9765; a pre-registered cross-family blind recheck (GPT-5.5 vs the Anthropic-line panel, 81.5% vs a registered 95% bar, 29/40 on planted controls, κ ≈ 0.45 reported beside the raw agreement "because raw agreement overstates at this base rate") FAILED its bar, localized the disagreement into **six named seam rulings** (R1 DEM accept / R2 RELPREC strict / R3 EVAL operational / R4 CONT split / R5 IDENT strict / R6 QUAL strict — each recording a `seam_applied` code so the seam population of future corrections is *measured, not guessed*), the 565 flagged items were re-judged blind under the ruled contract (186 verdicts changed), the record re-priced through the identical estimator (0.9765 → 0.9110), and the ruled contract's own cross-family agreement was re-measured in an addendum (κ = 0.4271 three-category / 0.4723 decisive-only — dual convention, both reported; residual divergence stated asymmetric: lenient against the stricter readings). Meta-rule: "a correction that needs a different reading changes this document first, by the same pre-registered discipline, never mid-run."

**Prior-art verdict (searched this session, see §5):** class saturated (gold noise distorts benchmarks: Northcutt arXiv:2103.14749; ReDocRED arXiv:2205.12696 measures ~65% missing gold; Klie arXiv:2405.11919 owns CI'd annotation-error sampling; Nahum arXiv:2410.18889 machine-adjudicates gold + rescores), **composition unpublished**: sampled machine-adjudicated gold-support ceiling + two-ceiling accounting + per-gold-item closed-set fate census has no published whole (fate census: zero directional hits). Internal signal-diff: reflex's ceilings are spend/size ceilings; no repo prices gold quality. Not Super-GOAT (no runnable proof path today; needs a judge lane + a written contract first) → filed as **reflex Issue 108** with the contract doctrine riding it.

**Fusion (the ladder's two-sided check):** `grammar-not-answers` × ceiling probe × decision-index-beat. The first guards ingestion contamination, the second guards score interpretation, the third consumes both on the JDI. Decision-index-beat's B5 law already says board rank ≠ our-suite fit; the ceiling probe adds "and neither board nor our-suite scores are gold-absolute — price the gold first." Consumer check (rulewright): a ceiling probe on the clippy corpus's labeled eval fixtures would price how many fixture labels are ambiguous as-stated before reading retrieval floors — noted in Issue 108 as a secondary consumer; the primary is the harness.

**Harness adaptation (defined in Issue 108, not here):** classification suites have no enumeration stage, so the OntoSmith fate set does not transplant verbatim — the issue defines the harness shapes: coverage ceiling = the gate's SERVED share at its operating point (abstention is the reachability cut); fate census = {correct/wrong × supported/not_supported, abstained, unknown} with the `correct ∧ not_supported` cell (lane agrees with unsupported gold) as the strongest new signal (shared bias / memorization); ceiling-adjusted score = a per-item JOIN on the supported subset, never a division (lanes can agree with unsupported gold); plus a pre-registered judge-validity gate (planted-flip catch bar — their own ruled contract still measured κ ≈ 0.43, so judge noise is effect-sized) and a no-self-judging law (the adjudicating judge must not be a scored entrant on the suite it adjudicates — a hosted lane can resolve to jev itself).

### 2.2 Threshold-stability audit (→ reflex Issue 109)

`pipeline/threshold_stability.py`: bootstrap the threshold-tuning procedure on dev (200 resamples) → distribution of t* (stable selection = tight distribution, p5/p95 reported) + held-out F1(t) curve around t* ("robust optimum = flat region, not a knife's edge"). Companions in `operating_points.py`: three production operating points (max-F1 / precision-constrained ≥0.95 "graph contamination is costlier than misses" / routed two-threshold minimizing review rate s.t. auto precision ≥ 0.90) with a **vacuous-precision guard** (n_accept < max(10, 5% of dev) invalid). Reflex fits fused-gate thresholds per suite (cal ρ=30) and rethink pins per-suite `ESC_GATE_RHOS` — neither audits whether the fitted point is stable or on a plateau. Offline, cheap, rides stored cal records → **reflex Issue 109**.
