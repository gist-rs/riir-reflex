# Issue 100 — Inductive-bias lane-seating diagnostics (robustness delta + label-irregularity probe)

**Status:** OPEN — filed from `riir-reflex/.research/012_Tabular_Inductive_Bias_Lane_Seating.md` (arXiv:2207.08815 distill). REPORT-ONLY harness probes; no serving-path change; byte-identical boots with the flags unset.

The serving board records modelless-vs-encoder outcomes per suite as data; the research note supplies the mechanism-level theory (smooth-bias mismatch, uninformative-feature robustness, orientation preservation) and this issue turns it into two cheap measurements. Goal: two numbers per suite that predict which lane to seat — the McElfresh-style property screen (arXiv:2305.02997) applied to our board. Either probe may come back flat; a flat result is a recorded negative and the issue closes on it.

- [ ] **T1 — `--robust-ab`: cross-lane robustness delta.** Reuse `echo_gates::corrupt_state` + the OOD rung ladder, but evaluate BOTH lanes at every rung (modelless floor vs `--laya`/`--d1`/`--mammoth`/`--drex` whichever lanes the run arms) and report per-suite paired degradation deltas (corrupted − clean, per lane) + the delta-of-deltas with LB95. Suite coverage MUST include at least one DEAD-encoder suite (banking77 or emotion) and one seated-encoder suite (xnli_en or ag_news) — a live head-to-head on a DEAD suite is otherwise impossible to interpret (verdict-round-1 caveat). Prediction from finding 2 (extrapolated — the paper measured MLP-vs-tree, not kNN): modelless degrades less on lexically-clustered suites. Predicates: single-arm echo gates untouched; REPORT-ONLY output columns; no gate/exit-code coupling.
- [ ] **T2 — `--label-smooth-ab`: label-irregularity diagnostic (finding-1 analog).** Graph-smooth each corpus row's training label toward its embedding nearest neighbors (sigmoid-weighted over cosine similarity — never softmax) at a few mixing strengths; re-evaluate both lanes on the smoothed corpus. Prediction (inverse of the paper's figure): smoothing toward neighborhood consensus helps the smooth-bias encoder more than the piecewise-constant modelless floor on irregular suites, and hurts it less on semantic ones. Deterministic (seeded like the corruption stream, SplitMix64 via the substrate path); REPORT-ONLY.

If not separable: record the negative in this file, close the issue, leave the note's theory section as-is.
